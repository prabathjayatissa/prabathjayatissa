## About Me

I am a medical doctor, health-informatics researcher, and academic working at the intersection of **healthcare, biomedical informatics, data science, and software engineering**.

My work focuses on making health information more interoperable, reusable, trustworthy, and useful for clinical care and research. I am particularly interested in translating healthcare standards and real-world clinical data into practical digital-health solutions.

- 🎓 **Academic focus:** Biomedical Informatics, Health Informatics, and digital health
- 🏥 **Healthcare informatics:** Electronic health records, clinical information systems, and health-data interoperability
- 🔗 **Standards & integration:** HL7 FHIR, HL7 CDA, OMOP Common Data Model, SNOMED CT, and IHE profiles
- 🧬 **Biomedical research:** Bioinformatics, computational drug discovery, molecular docking, and molecular dynamics
- 🤖 **Data & AI:** Python-based analytics, data engineering, automation, and applied AI
- 👨‍🏫 **Teaching:** Health information systems, interoperability, biomedical data science, and computational methods

Based in **Austria**.
